# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone who uses it or contributes to it, whatever their age, background, experience, gender identity, nationality, religion or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient with others, including people new to the topic.
- Give and accept feedback on code and ideas, never on the person.
- Credit other people's work and follow the academic integrity rules of the course.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Copying other teams' code, results or reports and presenting them as your own.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through a GitHub issue or a private message to any team member listed in the [README](README.md). The maintainers will review every report and may remove comments, commits or contributors that break this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
